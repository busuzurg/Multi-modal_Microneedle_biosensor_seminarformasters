1. Guard Ring & Split Ground Plane (`AGND` vs `DGND`)

Because the Glucose Oxidase reaction produces only 5 to 500 nA, digital switching noise from the 2.4 GHz BLE radio would corrupt the reading. The PCB splits the internal copper layer into an isolated Analog Ground (`AGND`) on the left and Digital Ground (`DGND`) on the right, joined at a single star-point via Ferrite Bead `FB1`.

2. True QFN-48 (`AD5941`) & aQFN-94 (`nRF5340`) Pinouts

`U2` (`AD5941`) uses pins 2, 3, and 4 for the primary 3-electrode cell (`CE0`, `RE0`, `SE0`) and pins 7–8 (`AIN0/AIN1`) to multiplex Lactate and Ketone microneedles. It communicates over an 8 MHz SPI bus (`P0.08–P0.11`) with `U1` (`nRF5340`).

3. Sub-Microamp Power Budget (14-Day Continuous Life)

`U4` (`BQ25120A`) regulates the `LIR2032` (45 mAh) coin cell down to `+1.8V_SYS` with a quiescent current of just 700 nA. `U2`'s Low-Power TIA (`LPTIA`) consumes only 10 µA in continuous bias mode, allowing 14+ days of operation per charge.

4. Bottom-Side Optical Aperture for `MAX30102` (`U3`)

`U3` (`MAX30102`) is reflow-soldered over a laser-milled optical cutout in the PCB so its 660 nm Red and 880 nm IR LEDs shine directly through the transparent PDMS microneedle substrate onto the skin's capillary bed.
